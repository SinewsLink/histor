# Governance of the Histor Specification
- **Now (v0.x):** SinewsLink Inc. edits the spec in the open. Changes are proposed as pull requests labelled `spec`, with a written rationale and the don't-miss checklist updated.
- **Neutrality commitment:** the spec name, schema `$id`s and namespaces aren't SinewsLink-branded. Any vendor may implement, extend (`x-<vendor>`) and claim conformance levels without permission or fees.
- **Trigger for neutral governance:** when at least two organisations other than SinewsLink ship Histor emitters or verifiers in production, the spec moves to a neutral home (an open-source foundation working group, or an OpenTelemetry semantic-conventions proposal) with a multi-vendor editors group.
- **Compatibility promise:** within a major version, changes are additive only (spec §13).
